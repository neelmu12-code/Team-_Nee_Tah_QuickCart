# Overall quality and residual risk

The testing provides substantial evidence about ordinary pricing and ordering behaviour, model constraints, input partitions, rounding, and payment/inventory interactions. Exact expected values, checks on real inventory, parameterized examples, Mockito interaction checks, and broad generated inputs complement each other. Confidence is strongest in the behaviours explicitly asserted by those tests. The implementation is not ready to be described as specification-compliant: significant monetary and inventory defects remain observable.

## What the measured evidence establishes

- The historical [coverage snapshot](coverage/README.md), taken November 10, 2026, records 50 passing tests with 77/77 executable lines and 52/52 branches covered. Coverage concerns the normal passing suite and excludes correct specification regressions tagged `known-defect`.
- The historical [mutation comparison](mutation/README.md) records 50 passing tests before and 51 after its two test additions, with 27/31 generated mutants killed in both runs (87.1%). Three survivors correct defective boundary comparisons; the fourth is equivalent for observable quote values. An unchanged score is explained rather than concealed.
- The saved specification regressions record 11 failing invocations, and the later multi-product decline regression records another failing invocation. These are defect evidence, not tool failures. Multiple invocations can represent one underlying defect, such as the tax error across three provinces.
- The current prospective [AI evaluation](ai/evaluation.md) is separate from those historical reports. Its control passed 51 tests and the reviewed treatment passed 52; both had 77/77 lines and 52/52 branches covered and killed the same 27/31 mutants. No additional coverage, mutant kills, or newly discovered root defects are credited. A separate current diagnostic run has 14 expected failures: 12 existing cases and two generated cases. Its own retained inputs, candidate output, initial compatibility error, review, and measured results support these claims; the historical mutation before/after runs already included the older AI-labelled tests.

These snapshots retain their original dates and suite sizes. They should not be presented as the final test count after later test additions. A high line/branch percentage measures execution, while PIT measures sensitivity to its generated changes; neither proves all business rules are correct.

## Remaining weaknesses and next testing priorities

1. **Known specification violations remain.** Bulk/free-shipping equality, coupon date inclusivity, the tax base, exact-stock acceptance, and payment/reservation order have failing regression evidence. Report them clearly. If a future implementation task authorizes fixes, correct them and bring their regressions into the normal passing suite before remeasuring adequacy.
2. **Repeated cart lines for the same SKU need investigation.** Source inspection suggests independent availability checks can accept each line even when combined demand exceeds stock; a later reservation could fail after an earlier reservation has changed stock. This is a source-derived risk, not a defect established by the saved runs, and duplicate-line semantics should be clarified.
3. **Dependency failures have no explicit contract.** Payment-gateway exceptions, reservation exceptions, and guarantees after partial failure are not specified or established by the suite. Define expected behaviour before asserting transactional recovery guarantees.
4. **Generated properties have limits.** They cover selected finite price/quantity/coupon ranges and use fixed reproducible seeds. Relational properties do not independently prove exact formulas, coupon endpoints, or interaction order.
5. **Assertion strength varies.** A positive-total smoke assertion can pass despite incorrect components. Strong exact-value and state/interaction assertions are the more persuasive evidence. Review AI-generated assertions against the specification and existing tests before adoption.
6. **The mutation campaign has limited scope.** Default operators generated 31 mutants in three production classes. This does not establish sensitivity to every possible BigDecimal formula error, model fault, dependency failure, or transaction-order defect.

With more time, first investigate duplicate-SKU and failure-state behaviour, clarify their contracts, and strengthen behavioural oracles where they add information. Additional ordinary examples solely to maintain a coverage percentage would contribute less confidence. The [two selected defects](defects.md), [testing strategy](strategy.md), and linked reports support the Evidence Sheet and recorded demonstration, which the team is preparing separately.
